Recommended Insurance Program

Property Recommended

Current: Citizens Wind + Lloyd's X-Wind

Proposed: Frontline


  • Single Carrier Solution Combines the current Citizens Wind and Lloyd's X-Wind structure into a single carrier solution, simplifying administration and claims handling.
  • Higher Building Valuation Frontline provides increased insured values compared to the expiring property program.
  • Equipment Breakdown Included Coverage is included for certain sudden and accidental breakdowns of covered building systems and equipment.
  • Special Form Coverage Broad property coverage designed to cover direct physical loss unless specifically excluded.
  • Property Premium Reduced Overall property premium is lower despite consolidating the program under one carrier.

General Liability Recommended

Current: Richmond National

Proposed: Westchester / Chubb


  • $1,000,000 Occurrence Limit
  • $2,000,000 General Aggregate
  • Medical Payments Included Adds $5,000 of Medical Payments coverage which was excluded under the current policy.
  • Assault & Battery Coverage Added The current program excludes Assault & Battery coverage. The proposed Westchester policy includes this coverage, providing a meaningful enhancement for a residential condominium association.
  • Hired & Non-Owned Auto Included Provides liability coverage for certain vehicle exposures arising out of association operations.
  • Premium Savings General Liability premium is reduced by approximately $3,600 annually while improving coverage.
  • Chubb-Affiliated Carrier Westchester is part of the Chubb organization, one of the largest commercial insurance groups worldwide.

Umbrella Liability New

  • $5,000,000 Liability Limit Adds an additional $5,000,000 layer of liability protection above the association's underlying insurance program.
  • Carrier: Midvale Indemnity
  • Annual Premium: $1,728
  • Sits Above Multiple Policies The umbrella sits above General Liability, Hired & Non-Owned Auto, Workers Compensation Employers Liability, and Directors & Officers coverage.
  • Total Liability Protection Increased Increases the association's available liability protection from approximately $1,000,000 to $6,000,000.
  • Critical for Habitational Risks Condominium associations can face severe liability claims that exceed primary policy limits. This umbrella provides an additional layer of protection when those losses occur.

Crime & Fidelity New

  • Employee Theft: $200,000 Protects association funds from theft committed by covered employees.
  • Computer Fraud: $100,000 Protects against theft of association funds caused by unauthorized computer activity.
  • Funds Transfer Fraud: $100,000 Protects against fraudulent instructions directing association funds to unauthorized accounts.
  • Fraudulent Inducement: $25,000 Provides limited protection when funds are transferred based on fraudulent misrepresentations.
  • Annual Premium: $581

Directors & Officers

  • Carrier: Philadelphia Indemnity
  • Limit: $1,000,000
  • Premium: $4,468
  • No Changes Recommended

Flood Coverage

Status: Awaiting Policy

Broker Recommendation

Based on our review of the available options, we recommend moving forward with the proposed insurance program.


The revised structure consolidates the property program into a single carrier, reduces General Liability costs, adds Assault & Battery coverage, introduces a $5,000,000 Umbrella Liability policy, adds dedicated Crime & Fidelity protection, and maintains the current Directors & Officers program.


Overall, the proposed structure provides broader liability protection, improved protection against theft and fraud, simplified program administration, and a lower overall insurance spend for the association.
