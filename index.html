Broker Recommendation

Based on our comprehensive review of available options, we strongly recommend moving forward with the proposed insurance program for Jamaica Manor, Inc.

The revised structure consolidates your property program into a single carrier, reduces General Liability costs by $3,600 annually, adds critical Assault & Battery coverage, introduces a $5,000,000 Umbrella Liability policy, adds dedicated Crime & Fidelity protection, and maintains your current Directors & Officers program.

Overall, the proposed structure delivers:

  • Broader liability protection – Increased from $1M to $6M across all exposures
  • Enhanced theft & fraud protection – New Crime & Fidelity coverage addresses cybersecurity risks
  • Simplified administration – Single carrier property program reduces complexity
  • Lower overall cost – $1,461 annual savings despite expanded coverage
